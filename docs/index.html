After Dark / ESPHome

Put the After Dark screen savers on an ESP32 with a little screen, and pick one from Home Assistant. Switch on the modules you want, check they fit the flash, and copy the program into ESPHome. Only the modules you switch on go into the firmware.

Each saver is ported from its page on the After Dark site. On a 1-bit OLED its art is shrunk and redrawn in black and white; on a character LCD it is drawn with the panel's eight custom characters. Hatched modules have no version for this display.

06Install with Vome

If your Home Assistant is connected to Vome, your AI assistant (Claude, Cursor, or any app that talks MCP) can put this on the board for you. It adds the config to ESPHome Device Builder, builds the firmware and installs it, and you approve each step.

  1. Add the Vome app to Home Assistant and make a key on its Agent tab, as Vome's ESPHome guide shows. Home Assistant needs the ESPHome Device Builder app too.
  2. For a new board, plug it into the machine running Home Assistant by USB. After the first install, updates go over Wi-Fi.
  3. Copy the request and paste it to your assistant.
The request carries the program above, so set up the board and display first.
